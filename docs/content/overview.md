# Overview of the meeting, a short goal/topic
type: section
active: true
title: Overview
---
Large language models and other language AI tools are rapidly changing how we do research: how we search and read the literature, write and review code, analyze data, communicate results, and teach the next generation of scientists. _Language AI in the Space Sciences_ brings together astronomers, space scientists, computer scientists, linguists, and educators to share progress, build tools together, and think critically about where this technology is taking our field.

Following the first two editions of the workshop in Baltimore, the third edition will be hosted by the [MPIA Data Science Group](https://www.mpia.de/institute/research-and-development/data-science) in Heidelberg, Germany, and is co-organized with the [European Space Agency (ESA)](https://www.esa.int) and the [NASA Astrophysics Data System (ADS)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu).

## <i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i> Topics

We welcome contributions on topics including, but not limited to:

* Language models for literature search, retrieval, summarization, and knowledge discovery
* AI research assistants and agents for scientific workflows
* Language AI for code, data analysis, and access to archives and observatories
* Benchmarks and evaluation of language models on scientific tasks
* Building, fine-tuning, and deploying domain-specific models for astronomy and space science
* **Mathematics** and mathematical reasoning with language AI
* **Linguistics** and the language of science
* **Ethics** of AI in research: authorship, reproducibility, bias, environmental cost, and responsible use
* **Teaching and learning** in the age of AI

## <i class="fa-solid fa-people-arrows"></i> Format

The workshop will combine invited and contributed presentations with tutorials and substantial unstructured time for hands-on work, discussion, and collaborative projects. We expect one day of tutorials followed by three days of conference sessions; the detailed programme will be announced later.
